The Problem

Why Most Traders Fail

90% of day traders fail — not because they lack knowledge, but because no one teaches them how to control the brain state from which they apply that knowledge. Fear, greed, overtrading, and emotional attachment to money aren't character flaws. They're brain states. And they can be trained.

Study Hypothesis

Day traders who learn to achieve and maintain an alpha brainwave state (8–12 Hz) will demonstrate reduced emotional reactivity, improved technical execution, shorter learning curves, and greater consistency — compared to their own pre-study baseline.

Training Framework

The Three Pillars

The study is built on three daily training practices, each targeting a different aspect of brainwave optimization for trading performance.

01
Muse Neurofeedback

Daily MUSE Training

10–15 minute biofeedback sessions using the Muse app. MIND sessions train focused attention through audio feedback — calm mind produces beach sounds, wandering mind triggers storms. STRENGTH sessions (Phase 2+, Athena only) train prefrontal cortex engagement for executive function and impulse control.

02
Pre-Market Meditation

CORE ALPHA Session

15–30 minute Trick Trades proprietary binaural-beat meditation before market open, recorded via Mind Monitor. This trains your brain to enter an alpha state intentionally and establishes a calm, focused baseline before you trade. Think of it as a pre-flight checklist for your mind.

03
Live EEG Monitoring

Trading with Your Brain Visible

Wear your Muse headband during every live trading session. Mind Monitor records your brainwave activity in real-time, creating a second-by-second map of your brain state alongside your trades. This data reveals what's happening between your ears at the moment of every decision.

90-Day Structure

The Journey

The study progresses through four distinct phases, each building on the last. Think of it as learning to walk before you run — we establish a foundation, then layer complexity.

Days -7 to -1

Preloading Phase

Set up your equipment, complete practice sessions, establish your brain snapshot baseline (3 min eyes-open + 3 min eyes-closed EEG recording), and finish the intake questionnaire. This is about getting comfortable with the tools before anything is at stake.

  • Device setup and Mind Monitor configuration
  • 2–3 practice meditation sessions
  • Baseline brain snapshot recording
  • Intake questionnaire and orientation
Days 1–30 — Foundation

Phase 1: Build the Baseline

Paper trade Days 1–5 to embed the feeling of calm, objective trading without financial fear. Live trading begins Day 6 with one contract. Daily CORE ALPHA + MUSE MIND training. Weekly EEG data submission to Dr. Krigolson.

  • Paper trading baseline (Days 1–5) — train your brain without financial pressure
  • Live trading begins Day 6 with strict one-contract sizing
  • Establish daily CORE + MUSE routine
  • Weekly data submissions begin
Days 31–60 — Integration

Phase 2: Layer the Tools

Add MUSE STRENGTH training for executive function. Mid-study brain snapshot at Day 45. Focus on recognizing alpha-to-high-beta transitions in real-time — learning to catch yourself before a bad decision, not after.

  • STRENGTH training added (Athena headband users)
  • Mid-study brain snapshot and evaluation at Day 45
  • Focus on real-time state awareness during trading
  • Mid-study questionnaire
Days 61–90 — Mastery

Phase 3: Prove the Pattern

Full protocol execution with all three pillars active. Consistency is the goal. Final brain snapshot at Day 90, comprehensive data submission, and the final offboarding questionnaire. This is where the hypothesis is tested.

  • Full protocol with all training components active
  • Focus on consistency and pattern reinforcement
  • Final brain snapshot and data submission
  • Final offboarding questionnaire

Important

Things to Know

Financial Risk Disclosure

Day trading involves substantial risk of financial loss. This study does not guarantee improved performance or profitability. You are trading with your own money. The standardized $2,500 account and one-contract sizing are designed to limit exposure, but losses are possible and should be expected as part of the learning process.

Your Data Is Protected

All EEG and performance data is de-identified before analysis. Your name never appears in data files shared with the research team. Published research uses only anonymized, aggregated data. You will not be individually identifiable unless you give separate written consent.

You Can Leave Anytime

Participation is voluntary. You can withdraw at any time, for any reason, without consequence to your Trick Trades membership. If you withdraw, data already submitted and anonymized may be retained, but identifiable data will be removed at your request.

Compliance Matters

The quality of the research depends on consistent participation. We ask for a minimum 80% compliance rate across all daily protocol elements. If you're struggling to keep up, reach out — we'd rather help you stay on track than lose your data.
